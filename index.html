Infrastructure Services

Network & Security Services

Cybersecurity Services

Endpoint and email protection, vulnerability scanning, penetration testing tooling and digital forensics — the layer that catches what the perimeter misses.

Managed IT Services

Consultancy

About Jesko Technology

Jesko Technology is an IT services and consulting company that helps businesses plan, build and secure the technology their operations depend on — from the network core to the applications end users touch every day.

Rather than selling a single product, we work across infrastructure, network security, cybersecurity and managed services, drawing on partnerships with the vendors that make up the enterprise technology stack. That means the recommendation we make is the one that fits your environment, not the one we happen to sell.

We work with organizations that need infrastructure and security handled properly, so their teams can focus on running the business instead of the systems underneath it.
