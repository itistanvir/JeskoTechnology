About Jesko Technology

Jesko Technology is a Dhaka-based IT services and consulting company that helps organizations plan, build and secure the technology their business runs on — from the network core to the applications their people use every day.

We work across infrastructure, network security, cybersecurity and managed services, backed by partnerships with the vendors that make up the modern enterprise technology stack. That breadth means the recommendation we make is shaped by what fits your environment, not by what we happen to sell.

Our approach combines hands-on technical expertise with independent, outcome-focused advice — so the organizations we work with can focus on running their business, while we handle the infrastructure and security underneath it.
